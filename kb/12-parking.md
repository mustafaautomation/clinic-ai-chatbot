Grove campus parking is the lot behind 418 Cedar Lane, entered from Elm Street. River campus parking is the garage under 22 Willow Street.

Accessible spaces are closest to each entrance. The desk can meet a patient with a wheelchair at the curb if you call ahead.

Please leave the fire lane clear. The lot is free for patients during a scheduled visit.
