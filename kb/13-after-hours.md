---
title: After-hours care
kicker: When the desk is closed
slug: after-hours
---

After 5:00 p.m. on weekdays, and all day Sunday, the clinic phones go to a recorded message. That message is not a nurse line.

For a new symptom that can wait until morning, request a callback for the next business day. For chest pain, trouble breathing, or stroke signs, call 911 instead of waiting.

The guide cannot page a clinician after hours and cannot tell you whether a symptom is safe to wait.
