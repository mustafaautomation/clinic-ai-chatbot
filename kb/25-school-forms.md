---
title: School and camp forms
kicker: Signatures
slug: school-forms
---

School, sports, and camp forms are signed by a clinician during a visit when the form matches a current exam.

Drop the form at the Grove desk or bring it to the appointment. The guide cannot sign a form or say that a child is cleared for sports.

Allow three business days after the visit if the form has to be reviewed by the clinician who saw the patient.
