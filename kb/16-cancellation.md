---
title: Cancellation policy
kicker: Changing a visit
slug: cancellation
---

You can cancel or move a visit in this chat with the confirmation code. Please do it at least two hours before the visit so the time can be offered to someone else.

A repeated same-day cancellation may need a phone call with the desk before the next booking.

Cancelling does not require a reason, a diagnosis, or a member ID.
