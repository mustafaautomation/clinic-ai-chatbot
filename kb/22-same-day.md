---
title: Same-day sick visits
kicker: When you need to be seen soon
slug: same-day
---

Same-day visits are follow-up or acute openings left on the schedule. The guide can show what is still open. It cannot triage how sick someone is.

If the schedule is full, the desk can add you to a callback list. That list is not a promise of a visit today.

Trouble breathing, chest pain, or a drooping face is an emergency. Call 911 rather than asking for a same-day slot.
