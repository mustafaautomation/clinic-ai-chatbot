---
title: Prescription pickup
kicker: After a clinician approves a refill
slug: prescription-pickup
---

A refill ticket in this chat is not a prescription and is never approved in the conversation. After a clinician approves it on their own time, the pharmacy fills it.

The guide cannot see whether a pharmacy has the medicine ready.

Controlled medications are not sent from chat. The care team may require an in-person visit.
